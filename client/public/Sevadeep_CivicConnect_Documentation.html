1. Executive Summary & What Has Been Done

Sevadeep CivicConnect is a full-stack, enterprise-grade civic reporting, community engagement, and environmental mobilization web platform engineered specifically for Sevadeep NGO in Pune, Maharashtra.

Unlike traditional static NGO brochures or government grievance portals that suffer from lack of transparency, CivicConnect bridges the gap between citizen grievance reporting, volunteer mobilization, coordinator triage, and municipal escalation with the Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC).

Key Deliverables Completed:

  • Full-Stack Architecture: React 18, Vite, and Tailwind CSS frontend coupled with a Node.js/Express REST API and MySQL 8.0 relational database with 11 tables.
  • Geospatial Issue Reporting: Point-and-click OpenStreetMap picker centered on Pune with automatic landmark suggestions and photo uploads.
  • Three-Tier Role-Based Access Control: Community Member (Citizen), Sevadeep Volunteer, and Sevadeep Coordinator / Admin.
  • Dual Ground-Truth Resolution Loop: Coordinators verify and mark issues resolved, while the original citizen reporter verifies the on-ground outcome with a 1–5 star rating.
  • Live Impact Telemetry: Real database aggregation of kilograms of waste collected, saplings planted, active volunteer hours, and PMC referral tracking.

3. Database Design (11 Relational Tables)

The database sevadeep_civicconnect contains 11 normalized tables with full referential integrity:

  • users: Registered accounts, hashed passwords, roles (citizen, volunteer, coordinator, admin), service hours, and points.
  • reports: Grievance details, coordinates (lat/lng), status, category, severity, PMC docket reference, and citizen rating.
  • report_photographs: Evidence photographs tagged as initial, progress, or resolution.
  • status_history: Immutable audit timeline of every status transition with coordinator notes and timestamps.
  • community_confirmations: Citizen upvotes preventing duplicate votes via composite primary keys.
  • comments: Public discussion stream with coordinator moderation flags (is_hidden, moderation_reason).
  • events: Community cleanup and tree plantation drives with volunteer slot limits and recorded impact metrics.
  • event_registrations: Volunteer enrollments with verified attendance flags and service hours awarded.
  • volunteer_assignments: Field assignments dispatched by coordinators.
  • notifications: In-app alert queue for report approvals, task assignments, and resolution requests.
  • audit_logs: Administrative action logs capturing actor ID, action type, IP address, and payload.

  2. Coordinator Triage & Approval: Log in as Coordinator → Open Admin Dashboard → Incoming Triage → Click "View & Triage" → In the Coordinator Decision Panel at the top right, select "Approved (Public on Map)" → Click Save. The report is now visible on the public map.

Volunteer Drive Completion & Impact Logging: Log in as Volunteer → Go to Volunteer Activities → Register for a drive → Switch to Coordinator → Open Admin Activities → Click "Mark Completed & Log Impact" → Input 520 kg waste collected and 40 trees planted → Save. 6. Novelty & Distinctive Innovations

Standard Civic / NGO Portals Sevadeep CivicConnect Innovation Real-World Value
Unilateral Ticket Closure: Authorities close tickets without resident sign-off. Dual Ground-Truth Verification Loop: Coordinator signs off, but original reporter rates outcome (1–5 stars). Prevents paper-only resolutions and fosters authentic trust.
Static Annual PDF Reports: Metrics published once a year without live proof. Live Impact Telemetry: Real-time database aggregation of kg waste, trees planted, and volunteer hours. Radical transparency for donors, citizens, and municipal partners.
Black-Box Municipal Blame: Out-of-scope issues are dropped. Formal PMC Escalation Bridge: Issues requiring municipal equipment receive official PMC docket references tracked publicly. Honest civic advocacy without claiming false credit or abandoning residents.
Privacy Leaks: Phone numbers and exact house addresses exposed. Differential Privacy Redaction: General landmark coordinates only; phone numbers and home addresses completely shielded. Protects citizen reporters from harassment or retaliation.

7. Project Scope & Future Roadmap

Current Operational Footprint: Pune Metropolitan Region (Shivajinagar, Swargate, Kothrud, Deccan Gymkhana, Mandai, Vetal Tekdi, Kasba Peth, Hadapsar). Handles solid waste management, drinking water leakage, and public parks.

Future Enhancements:

  • WhatsApp Business API bot integration for chat-based photo and location reporting.
  • AI computer vision model for automatic waste volume estimation and duplicate detection within 50 meters.
  • Automated two-way integration with the Pune Municipal Corporation (PMC) Pune Connect grievance API.
  • Progressive Web App (PWA) offline caching for volunteers operating on hill trails (Tekdis).

8. Social & Environmental Impact

  • Empirical Environmental Metrics: Measures exact kilograms of plastics and debris cleared from riverbanks and hill slopes, producing actionable data for municipal policy.
  • Community Solidarity: Confirmation upvotes empower neighborhoods to highlight chronic sanitation blackspots.
  • Certified Volunteer Hours: Volunteers earn authenticated service credentials suitable for university credits and corporate CSR recognition.
  • Data-Driven Civic Accountability: Chronic dumping heatmaps provide objective evidence for Ward Officers to install streetlights and waste containers.
